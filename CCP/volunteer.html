Community Cyber Defense Program

National Cyber Crime Volunteer Program

An initiative by the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) bringing together patriotic citizens, cyber security enthusiasts, and legal experts to assist law enforcement agencies in making Indian cyberspace resilient, safe, and lawful.

Category 1

Cyber Volunteer Unlawful Content Flagger

Volunteers identify and submit public URLs of unlawful online content for emergency takedown under Section 69A of the IT Act:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) & Rape Imagery
  • Terrorism, radicalization & anti-national propaganda
  • Communal disharmony & hate incitement
Category 2

Cyber Awareness Promoter

Volunteers organize community workshops, distribute digital safety pamphlets, and educate vulnerable groups:

  • Conducting school & college cyber hygiene sessions
  • Spreading awareness on the 1930 Financial Helpline
  • Educating senior citizens on UPI QR & OTP fraud traps
Category 3

Cyber Security Expert

Technical specialists collaborating on advanced threat vectors and forensic analysis:

  • Malware & APK reverse engineering
  • Cryptocurrency transaction trace analysis
  • Dark web threat monitoring & forensic research
