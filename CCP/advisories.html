Official Advisories & Threat Alerts

Authoritative warning bulletins issued by the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), MHA, regarding active fraud campaigns.

High Severity Threat Ref: I4C/ADV/2026/08

Public Advisory on "Digital Arrest" & Law Enforcement Impersonation Scams

Scammers posing as CBI, Mumbai Police, Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB), ED, or High Court officials are placing video calls over Skype/WhatsApp and threatening citizens with fabricated parcel confiscations containing illicit narcotics. Citizens are coerced into remaining on camera under "Digital Arrest" and transferring savings into fraudulent "RBI verification accounts".

Official Stance: Indian law enforcement agencies NEVER conduct arrests over video calls nor request citizens to transfer funds to any bank account for judicial verification.
High Severity Threat Ref: I4C/ADV/2026/04

Advisory on Fake Summon Emails Issued in the Name of Cyber Crime Police & MHA

Fraudsters are circulating forged court summon emails with headers resembling Delhi Police, CBI, or INTERPOL, alleging that the recipient's IP was implicated in child pornography offenses. The email demands a fine payment within 24 hours to avoid arrest.

Official Stance: Do NOT reply or pay fines to such emails. Authentic police notices are never served with arbitrary QR codes or personal bank account payment links.
Moderate Severity Ref: I4C/ADV/2026/02

Advisory on Fake Electricity Power Disconnection SMS & APK Malware

SMS alerts threatening power disconnection tonight due to unpaid electricity bills are widespread. Citizens are asked to contact a mobile number where scammers guide them to install a malicious APK file (e.g. QuickSupport or ElectricityPay.apk) which hijacks banking SMS and OTPs.

Official Stance: Electricity discoms never send disconnection threats from personal 10-digit mobile numbers. Always pay utility bills via official electricity board portals.
Administrative Notice Ref: I4C/NOT/2026/01

Important Public Notice regarding CPGRAMS Portal

The Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System (CPGRAMS) is dedicated to administrative grievances against government departments. Cyber crime complaints and bank lien requests must be filed exclusively on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCRP / 1930) for statutory police action and fast-track financial freeze.
