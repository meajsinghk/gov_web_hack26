Citizen Defense Guidelines

8 Essential Cyber Safety Guidelines

Financial Protection

1. Banking, UPI & QR Codes

  • Never enter UPI PIN to receive money. Entering a PIN only deducts money.
  • Do not scan QR codes sent on WhatsApp for marketplace sales.
  • Bank officials never ask for CVV, OTP, or NetBanking passwords over phone calls.
Identity Protection

2. Social Media & Impersonation

  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via Authenticator apps on Instagram, Facebook & WhatsApp.
  • Beware of emergency distress messages from cloned friend accounts demanding immediate UPI cash.
  • Never accept video calls from unknown foreign numbers on WhatsApp.
Special Safeguards

3. Child & Women Online Safety

  • Report non-consensual image morphing immediately on NCRP for emergency 69A IT Act takedowns.
  • Educate children never to share personal webcam feeds or school locations in gaming lobbies.
  • Do not succumb to cyber blackmail threats; preserve screenshots and contact 1930.
Device Integrity

4. Mobile Devices & APK Safety

  • Never install APK files sent on WhatsApp claiming to be electricity bills, e-Challans, or KYC updates.
  • Never install remote screen-sharing apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk) on caller instructions.
  • Disable 'Install from Unknown Sources' in your Android security settings.
Investment Defense

5. Job & Investment Scams

  • Beware of Telegram part-time job groups offering ₹3,000/day for liking YouTube videos.
  • No SEBI-registered stockbroker guarantees 100% daily returns or uses Telegram VIP channels.
  • Never transfer funds into individual savings accounts for institutional IPO allocations.
Email Security

6. Email Phishing & Ransomware

  • Verify sender domain header. Genuine government notices originate from @gov.in or @nic.in.
  • Do not open ZIP/RAR email attachments claiming to be court summons or tax refund notices.
  • Maintain offline encrypted backups of critical organizational databases.
Network Defense

7. Public Wi-Fi & USB Charging

  • Never conduct NetBanking or UPI transactions over open airport or railway public Wi-Fi networks.
  • Use USB data-blocker adapters when charging phones at public kiosks (Juice Jacking defense).
  • Always check for HTTPS padlock icon before entering login credentials.
Telecom Protection

8. SIM Swap & Aadhaar Hygiene

  • If your mobile suddenly shows 'No Service' without reason, contact telecom operator immediately.
  • Lock your Aadhaar biometrics on the official UIDAI portal / mAadhaar application.
  • Check active SIMs registered in your name on DoT's official TAFCOP portal.

Statutory Legal Framework

Key Provisions: IT Act 2000 & Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023

Summary of criminal sections applicable to cyber fraud, hacking, and online harassment.

Section / Act Cyber Offense Description Prescribed Penalties
Section 66D (IT Act) Cheating by personation using computer resource / digital device (UPI / Fake Call Phishing). Up to 3 years imprisonment + ₹1 Lakh fine
Section 67 & 67A (IT Act) Publishing or transmitting obscene or sexually explicit material in electronic form. Up to 5 years imprisonment + ₹10 Lakh fine
Section 67B (IT Act / POCSO) Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) creation, possession, or electronic transmission. Up to 7 years imprisonment + ₹10 Lakh fine
Section 66F (IT Act) Cyber Terrorism / Attacking Critical Information Infrastructure (CII). Life Imprisonment
Section 318 (BNS 2023) Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property (Read with Sec 66D IT Act). Up to 7 years imprisonment + Fine
