Transparent Citizen Workflow

How Your Report Protects Your Money

Clear 4-step statutory timeline from initial submission to inter-bank freeze and judicial refund.

1
Express Intake (< 60s)

Citizen enters amount and UTR or uploads screenshot parsed via on-device WebAssembly OCR.

2
1930 Bank Node Alert

Automated API signal pushed to beneficiary bank, wallet provider, and NPCI gateway.

3
Interim Account Lien

Beneficiary bank puts temporary hold on suspect mule account to prevent ATM withdrawal.

4
Court Refund Order

State Cyber Cell registers FIR and assists magistrate court in releasing frozen funds back to victim.
