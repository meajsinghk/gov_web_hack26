Central Board of Trustees (CBT), EPF

The CBT is a statutory tripartite body constituted under Section 5A of the EPF & MP Act, 1952.

  • Chairman: Union Minister for Labour and Employment (Ex-Officio).
  • Vice-Chairman: Minister of State for Labour and Employment (Ex-Officio).
  • Government Representatives: Nominees from Central Ministries and State Labour Departments.
  • Employers' Representatives: Nominees representing leading national trade & industry associations.
  • Employees' Representatives: Nominees representing major central trade union organizations.
  • Chief Executive Officer: Central Provident Fund Commissioner (CPFC).
