Statutory Schemes & Legal Framework

Parliamentary enactments, social security schemes, and international agreements administered by the Central Board of Trustees (CBT), EPFO.

1. The Employees' Provident Funds & Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952

Primary Act

Enacted to provide social security and institutional savings mechanism for the formal Indian workforce. Covers every factory and commercial establishment employing 20 or more persons.

  • Statutory coverage: Section 1(3)(a) and 1(3)(b) mandatory for 180+ notified industry classes.
  • Wage Ceiling: Mandatory for employees drawing basic pay up to ₹15,000 per month (voluntary above ceiling).
  • Tripartite Administration: Managed by Central Board of Trustees with Government, Worker, and Employer representation.
Read Full Act Gazette & Amendments ↗

2. The Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 1952 (EPF)

Retirement Corpus

Governs accumulation, annual interest declaration, and non-refundable advances for medical emergencies, marriage, education, and housing construction.

  • Contribution Matrix: Employee (12%) + Employer EPF share (3.67%).
  • Current Annual Interest Rate: 8.25% for FY 2025–26 credited monthly on compounding basis.
  • Advances: Form 31 non-refundable withdrawals without closing the active PF account.
View EPF Scheme Guidelines & Forms ↗

3. The Employees' Pension Scheme, 1995 (EPS)

Monthly Pension

Provides defined lifelong monthly pension upon retirement at age 58, as well as widow, child, and orphan pensions upon untimely demise of the wage earner.

  • Funding: 8.33% of employer contribution diverted to EPS + 1.16% central government budgetary grant.
  • Vesting Criteria: Minimum 10 years of pensionable service for superannuation pension.
  • Scheme Certificate: Enables accumulation of past service when transferring between employers.
View EPS 1995 Pensioner Regulations ↗

4. The Employees' Deposit-Linked Insurance Scheme, 1976 (EDLI)

Life Assurance

Free life insurance assurance for all active contributing EPF members, payable directly to the designated nominee or legal heirs without any deduction from employee salary.

  • Zero Cost to Worker: Entire 0.50% contribution borne by the employer.
  • Coverage Amount: Up to ₹7,00,000 (Statutory floor: ₹2,50,000).
  • Seamless Claim: Form 5IF / Form 20 online beneficiary settlement.
View EDLI Claim Settlement Procedure ↗

5. Employment Incentive Schemes & International Agreements (SSA)

Incentives & SSA

Government of India employment subsidy programs and bilateral Social Security Agreements with 20+ countries to prevent dual social security contributions for Indian expatriates.
