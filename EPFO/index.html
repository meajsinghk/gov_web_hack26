    Digital Citizen Pathways

    4-Pillar Unified Service Hub

    Streamlined categorization replacing scattered legacy links into direct statutory action pathways.

    Pillar 1: Member & Employee Services

    UAN Management, Passbook, PF Withdrawals & Transfers

    • Member Passbook & Balance Instant Login

      View and download your monthly PF statement, interest credits, and employer share.

    • Member Portal (UAN Login) Unified Portal

      Single window login for online claims, profile update, and passbook synchronization.

    • Activate Universal Account Number (UAN) First Time

      Activate your 12-digit UAN with registered Aadhaar and mobile number for online services.

    • Know Your UAN Status

      Locate your active UAN using Member ID, PAN, or Aadhaar credentials.

    • Online Claims & PF Advance (Form 19, 10C, 31, 10D) Paperless

      File Form 19 (Final Settlement), 10C (Pension Withdrawal), 31 (Advance / Illness / Housing), and 10D (Monthly Pension).

    • Transfer Request (One Member One EPF - OICP)

      Consolidate previous PF accounts seamlessly into your current active UAN.

    • Update KYC & Aadhaar / PAN Seeding

      Link bank account IFSC, PAN card, and Aadhaar to prevent TDS deduction on withdrawals.

    • Submit e-Nomination

      Register family nominees digitally for smooth disbursement of PF, Pension, and EDLI insurance.

    • File Death Claim (Form 20 / 5IF / 10D)

      Beneficiary / Legal heir pathway to claim accumulated PF balance and EDLI life insurance benefit.

    • International Workers (SSA Portal)

      Social Security Agreements (SSA) and Certificate of Coverage (CoC) for cross-border employees.

    Pillar 2: Pensioners (EPS Services)

    Life Certificates, PPO Inquiries & Monthly Pension Ledger

    • Jeevan Pramaan (Digital Life Certificate) Face Auth / Biometric

      Submit your annual Digital Life Certificate (DLC) online via Android mobile Face RD or biometric device.

    • Pension Payment Enquiry & PPO Details Official MIS

      Track monthly pension credit details, disbursing bank IFSC, and life certificate validity date.

    • Know Your Pension Payment Order (PPO) Number

      Locate your 12-digit PPO number using Bank Account Number or Member ID.

    • EPS Pension Entitlement Calculator

      Calculate estimated monthly pension benefit based on pensionable service and average salary.

    • Scheme Certificate Guidelines

      Retain pension service eligibility when switching jobs or before reaching 58 years of age.

    • Pensioner Forms, Circulars & Guidelines

      Download Life Certificate forms, bank mandate forms, and administrative EPS circulars.

    Pillar 3: Employers & Establishments

    ECR Challan Filing, Shram Suvidha & Compliance

    • Unified Employer Portal (ECR Login) Establishment Login

      Single-window interface for statutory return submission, challan generation, and member management.

    • Submit Electronic Challan cum Return (Revamped ECR) Monthly Return

      File monthly statutory contributions for EPF, EPS, EDLI, and administrative charges.

    • View / Download Payment Receipts & Challan TRRN

      Track statutory payment acknowledgments and TRRN reconciliation statements.

    • Shram Suvidha Portal (LIN Registration) M/o Labour Portal

      Register new commercial establishment, apply for Labour Identification Number (LIN), and file unified annual returns.

    • Employee Enrolment Campaign & Exit Date Marking

      Enrol new workforce and update Date of Exit for outgoing employees via employer DSC.

    • Exempted Establishments Performance Portal

      Compliance metrics, investment returns, and trust audit reports for exempted private PF trusts.

    • Employer Documents & Compliance Manuals

      Download DSC registration guidelines, ECR file format specifications, and inspection checklists.

    Pillar 4: Grievance & Citizen Assistance

    EPFiGMS 2.0, Nidhi Aapke Nikat & RTI Resolution

    • Register Grievance on EPFiGMS 2.0 Grievance Portal

      Official redressal system for delayed PF transfers, passbook discrepancy, pension arrears, or KYC issues.

    • View Grievance Status & Send Reminder Track Ticket

      Track resolution timeline using Grievance Registration Number and submit reminders for pending tickets.

    • Nidhi Aapke Nikat 2.0 (District Outreach Camps) Monthly Camp

      Locate monthly on-ground redressal camps conducted on the 27th of every month in every district across India.

    • Regional Office Directory & Desk Locator

      Find address, email, jurisdiction, and Public Relations Officer (PRO) details for 140+ EPFO offices.

    • RTI Online Portal & Proactive Disclosures

      File Right to Information requests and view Central Public Information Officer (CPIO) roster.

    • Comprehensive Citizen FAQs & Video Guides

      Official step-by-step answers and video tutorials on UAN, DLC submission, and tax rules.
